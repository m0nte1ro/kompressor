Experimental starting points for streaming-style quality. Test output before replacement; CRF and ICQ values are not equivalent.

Planning ranges are assumptions for storage estimates, not encoder limits or measured results. Quality-based output can fall outside them. Efficient audio retains all tracks/channels, copies efficient low-bitrate tracks, uses AAC for mono/stereo and E-AC3 up to 5.1, and copies larger layouts or unknown bitrates.
